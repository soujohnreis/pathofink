Privacy Policy — Path of Ink

Effective for the 1.3.7 monetization relaunch, prepared October 24, 2026. Developer: John Reis · Contact: dev.johnlenonreis@gmail.com

Earlier versions do not contain AdMob advertising or the coin wallet described below.

Progress, settings and cloud recovery

The game stores scores, Path progress, unlocked rewards, equipment, language, audio and accessibility settings on your device. If iCloud is available, profile progress is synchronized through Apple's CloudKit private database. A separate private wallet stores your coin balance, cosmetic ownership and a transaction ledger. Ledger entries use Apple transaction IDs or gameplay reward IDs to avoid duplicate credits. Purchases and coin spending require an online iCloud sync; earned rewards wait locally while offline. Coins do not expire.

Deleting the app removes local data and unsynchronized rewards. It does not delete your iCloud wallet or Apple purchase history. Reinstalling with the same iCloud account can recover the synchronized wallet and cosmetics. Contact the developer for help with deletion requests; Apple also provides controls for app data in iCloud settings. Do not delete cloud wallet data if you wish to recover it later.

Purchases and leaderboards

Apple processes Full Unlock and optional consumable coin purchases. The developer does not receive your card or payment details. StoreKit provides verified purchase information; CloudKit records fulfilled transaction IDs. A pseudonymous account token binds coin purchases to the intended iCloud wallet. Restore Purchases restores Full Unlock; consumables are recovered through the wallet rather than Apple's restore flow.

Game Center processes your player identifier and submitted scores for leaderboards. The developer uses these services for game functionality, not ad targeting.

Optional rewarded advertising

Players without Full Unlock may choose a Google AdMob rewarded ad for an eligible continue or result coin bonus. Ads play only when you choose to watch for a reward; they never interrupt active gameplay. Normal play and retry remain accessible when an ad is unavailable or declined. Full Unlock owners see no ads: they receive three extra starting lives in Climb and Daily (except One Life) and eligible result coin bonuses without watching an ad.

Google's Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform SDKs process information needed for consent, advertising, fraud prevention and measurement. This can include IP address (and approximate location derived from it), app/device information and app-scoped identifiers, ad views/interactions, crash logs and performance diagnostics. See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's privacy policy.

The game defaults to non-personalized advertising, does not request IDFA access, and does not send your wallet balance, cosmetic ownership, leaderboard identity, precise location, contacts or payment details to AdMob. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. Consent is requested where required before ad requests; use Privacy Options in the cosmetic shop to revisit available choices. The shop also links to Google's inappropriate-ad reporting form.

Your choices and children

You can decline optional ads, manage privacy options where available, avoid Game Center, or sign out of platform services in device settings. There is no separate third-party gameplay analytics SDK. The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts or precise location. It is not directed to children; it does not knowingly request personal information from children. Contact the developer if you believe a child's information has been provided.
